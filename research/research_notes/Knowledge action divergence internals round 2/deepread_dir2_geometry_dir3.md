#### 1. Manifold Steering Reveals the Shared Geometry of NN Representation and Behavior (arXiv 2605.05115; ICML 2026 per sweep D) — read HTML, first ~100k chars of 206k
- Spaces compared: activation manifold M_h (spline / thin-plate surface fit through PCA-64 activation centroids) vs behavior manifold M_y (spline through output probability distributions over concept tokens plus an "other" class, embedded in Hellinger space). Relation claimed: "scaled isometry" / "approximate Riemannian isometry" between geodesic distances on M_h and M_y. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05115)
- ISOMETRY CLAIM CHECK: the claim IS in the paper, not only in the LinkedIn summary. Geodesic-vs-geodesic correlation r = 0.99 weekdays, 0.89 months, 0.999 letters, 0.999 ages, 0.99 ICL 5x5 grid, 0.99 ICL 9x9 cylinder, 0.99 Mountain Car; linear (Euclidean) activation distance vs behavior geodesic is lower: 0.89, 0.53, 0.71, 0.36, 0.90, 0.81, 0.06. "About 2.8x lower energy" is also in the paper: cumulative Bhattacharyya energy manifold vs linear: weekdays 0.34 vs 0.93, months 0.36 vs 1.09, letters 2.42 vs 6.95, ages 5.21 vs 13.49 (p<0.001). Caveat: "isometry" is evidenced by a rank/Pearson correlation of distances, i.e. scaled and approximate, not metric equality. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05115)
- Models: Llama-3.1-8B (layer 28) only for language; a GRU (n=64) world model on Mountain Car. Nothing above 8B. Settings: cyclic/sequential concepts (weekdays, months, letters, ages), ICL graph tasks (after Park et al. 2025b). 16 prompts, K=50 intervention points. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05115)
- Metric validity: "naturalness" = distance of steered output to the model's own unintervened output-distribution manifold, NOT correctness; authors call it "one instantiation of a more general framework". No knowledge-action or correctness test. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05115)
- Authors' next steps (quoted): "Expanding experimental validation to more complex domains"; "Moving from token to sequence-level outputs"; "An unsupervised protocol would however significantly broaden the applicability of our methods"; "Manipulating intermediate algorithmic variables"; abstract concepts (refusal, sycophancy, persuasion) untested. Not done by anyone found in this read. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05115)
- Related work to check: Bigelow et al. 2025, Rodriguez et al. 2025, Sarfati et al. 2026 (linear-steering limits); Engels et al. 2024, Kantamneni & Tegmark 2025, Karkada et al. 2026 (curved concept geometry); Park et al. 2026 (toy softmax rep-to-output map, named closest work).

#### 2. Perfect Detection, Failed Control (arXiv 2606.24952) — read HTML
- Spaces compared: residual-stream detection direction (fake vs real entity; DiM, or hand-picked from lm_head rows of cautious vs confident openers) vs refusal-producing direction (built from lm_head rows of refusal vs compliant tokens). Relation: cosine angle; detection-causal alignment is said to hold for format (cos ~1, one lm_head direction detects and controls) and fail for hallucination→refusal. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24952)
- Numbers: cos(hand-picked detection, refusal)=0.12 (~83°; chance 1/sqrt(2304)≈0.02); cos(DiM detection, refusal)=−0.06; cross-model 0.12–0.20 (Gemma-2-2B 0.12, Llama-3.2-1B 0.20, Qwen-2.5-1.5B 0.16, Gemma-2-9B 0.13); base vs IT 0.1197 vs 0.1200. Linear probe detection AUC 1.000 from layer 5. ROT-15°, α=15, N=115: obvious fakes 40→73% refusal, subtle 13→60%, real-obscure correct 100→95%, FP 0/55→1/55. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24952)
- Caveat (important for interpretation): the refusal direction is built from lm_head (unembedding) rows, not from residual activations at refusal; the "83°" is thus partly detection-vs-unembedding geometry. Sample sizes "20–55 questions per condition". Max 9B.
- No nonlinear steering tried. Quoted next steps: "Whether this angle varies systematically across a broader range of behaviors"; "Whether the orthogonality persists when measured via nonlinear probes on larger models is an open question"; "replication on models above 10B and on non-decoder architectures remains needed"; "Whether it is specific to entity-centric questions or a general property of the output mapping is untested." None found done. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24952)
- Controls worth copying: format as a positive control where detect=control; 100 matched-norm random directions; 2000 random unit directions for detection signal (6.9x); base vs IT comparison.
- Evidence for D2: supports "the detect→act map is misaligned in the failing case but aligned in a control case". Does not show the map CHANGES for failing items vs succeeding items within one task. Reuse: COPY controls, ADAPT design (build refusal direction from activations, not lm_head).

#### 3. Decodable but Not Corrected by Fixed Residual-Stream Linear Steering (arXiv 2605.05715, Ming Liu) — read HTML first 100k of 148k

- Spaces compared: mode-specific contrastive vectors vs a shared incorrect-vs-correct direction; "specificity ratio" 0.119 (Llama) read as "88% of each contrastive vector aligns with a shared 'incorrect-vs-correct' direction"; Qwen 0.152; OT-vs-KD direction cos 0.86 with correctness direction (circularity check). Exact formula not in the part read. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05715)
- Linear steering: 29 configs across contrastive, probe-guided, multi-layer, rank-k (k=1,3,5), prompt-end; uniform shared −12.1pp; all else Δ≈0 (−1.7 to +1.6pp); Qwen Δ∈[−0.9,+0.8]. Mean-difference erasure −3.6pp; full oblique LEACE NOT tested. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05715)

Not done by anyone found.
- Controls worth copying: refusal-steering positive control (shows the steering pipeline works), 10 random erasures, TOST equivalence (±2.5pp) to claim a null properly, GroupKFold, length-regression baseline (71.3% — length alone nearly matches the probe: pitfall), label-noise simulation.

#### 4. KAPPA, Bridging the Knowledge-Prediction Gap on MCQs (arXiv 2509.23782; Park, Pyun, Jo; ICML 2026) — abstract only so far
- Claim: distinct knowledge vs prediction subspaces in residual stream; KAPPA aligns them at inference and "effectively reduces the gap across diverse MCQ benchmarks and models, and generalizes to free-form settings". Models, angles, numbers not yet read. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23782)
- UPDATE (full HTML v4 read, first 100k of 148k): Models: Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct main; TruthfulQA extras Mistral-7B-v0.3, Qwen3-4B, **Qwen3-14B (>8B)**; Qwen3-32B only briefly. Benchmarks BBH, MMLU, ARC-C, TruthfulQA, GSM8k, PubMedQA, BBQ. — [arXiv html v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782v4)
- Spaces compared: knowledge subspace = column span of a k-class linear probe trained on ground-truth labels; prediction subspace = span of a probe trained on the model's own greedy answer; same layer, final answer-prefix token. Relation: principal angles and orthogonal linear CKA. — [arXiv html v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782v4)
- Geometry: mean principal angle 60–80° mid-layers, rising to ~90° late (random ≈88.7°); CKA 0.4–0.8 (random ≲0.2). Gap (1−agreement) vs angle across 8 benchmarks: Llama ρ=0.976 (p=0.001), Qwen ρ=0.619 (p=0.106, n.s.). NOT analysed per item (gap vs aligned items) — only across benchmarks. Prediction subspace vs answer-token logit subspace ~65–70°. — [arXiv html v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782v4)
- Fix: closed-form affine minimal-ℓ2 update aligning prediction coordinates to knowledge coordinates each decoding step (α=30 one layer). Llama TruthfulQA acc 56.7→67.8 (knowledge probe 76.3); BBQ-Age 59.9→73.8; Qwen3-14B TruthfulQA 71.6→77.7; free-form small (TQA 41.7→44.2; GSM8k 91.6→90.7). Baselines CAA, DoLA; CAA sometimes competitive. Cross-dataset transfer mostly poor. — [arXiv html v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782v4)
- Quoted next steps: "Several important extensions remain, including more reliably extracting knowledge from hidden states", plus nonlinear knowledge, broader prompting, higher-dim interventions. Studies only linearly accessible knowledge.
- Evidence for D2: the strongest existing "geometry of knowledge vs action" result, and it shows a probe-to-probe linear (affine) repair WORKS in MCQ. Caveat: the "prediction subspace" is defined by the output, so alignment is partly circular; per-item geometry change untested. Reuse: COPY the two-probe principal-angle design; ADD per-item split (gap items vs aligned items). Scoop risk: high for MCQ.

#### 11. 2609.10739 Perfect Aliasing in Compliant-Context Truth Probes (Direction 2, single-turn)
- Gemma-2-9B (**>8B**; reward-trained RL policy, 3 seeds), Llama-3.1-8B instructed. Single-token game: report secret bit to ally, complement to rival. - Direct relevance to D2: a probe that flips sign under action-conflict is exactly "the readout-to-behavior map changes when the model acts against CRID"; but here it's a probe-validity artefact (label aliasing), not a geometry change. PITFALL for D2: any "geometry changes on failing items" result must fit probes on mixed contexts (where truth and action disagree) or it may measure the action, not the truth. #### 19. Lost in Distraction (OpenReview IrfhtJkhmV; Liang, Song, Chen, Cao, Han, Guo; COLM 2026 accept) — OpenReview page + reviews via Chrome
- DeepPlanning (240 multi-tool travel-planning cases, CN+EN); models incl. Qwen3.5-397B, Kimi K2.5, GPT-OSS-120B, Gemini 3 Flash (all **>8B**). Same-schema semantically-similar noise injected into tool observations. Recognition (Vcom/Vcom-dim, commonsense) stays roughly stable; hard-constraint adherence (Vpers) collapses: Qwen 0→10 noise 64.7→33.3 (Wilcoxon p=0.001, d=0.51); random-text and length-padding controls do NOT collapse (63.2/65.1), so it is semantic distraction, not length. Self-selection restores Qwen to 60.8 (p=0.006) but hurts GPT-OSS where recognition is unreliable. Noise also induces early termination (fewer tool calls/steps). — [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=IrfhtJkhmV)
- BEHAVIOR-ONLY. "Recognition" is measured behaviorally (selection / commonsense metrics), not from internals; authors promised to replace "vague claims such as the model 'knows but does not act'" with metric-level definitions (quoted from rebuttal). Dose-response is reported at 0/5/10 noise only — too coarse to tell gradual vs abrupt (0→5 p=0.071, 5→10 p=0.039 suggests graded). Reuse: COPY the isomorphic noise design + length-padding control; ADAPT as a multi-step act-against-CRID testbed for probes.

#### 20. ContextRotBench (OpenReview DiZ67nkfay; Lu, Gunukula, Agashe, Liu, Wang; CBW workshop poster) — OpenReview abstract via Chrome
- 213 base tasks, 17 repos, >1,000 controlled context-rot scenarios (coding agents). "context length alone is not a primary cause of context rot; instead, degradation is driven by the content and timing of accumulated task history"; attention analysis: "persistent instructions receive decreasing attention despite remaining in context, suggesting a mechanism for constraint drift." Models not on abstract. — [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=DiZ67nkfay)
- Mixed: behavior benchmark + attention (white-box but attention ≠ decodability). "Decreasing attention" suggests a gradual internal decline; whether the behavioral violation is a threshold crossing is not stated. Reuse: ADAPT (timing manipulation is the right lever for D3). Workshop.

### Null-result risk (D2): HIGH-MEDIUM
- The cleanest prior attempt at a nonlinear fix (2605.05715 MLP) got +2.8pp with near-equal fixes and damages. Confidence Manifold says correctness geometry is simple and linear. Spokes says "manifolds" may be discrete. So "manifold steering repairs what linear cannot" is likely to come out null or tiny. A geometry-change finding (map differs on failing items) is less likely to be null but risks being explained by aliasing (2609.10739) or length (2605.05715's length baseline 71.3% vs probe 71.6%).

### Scoop risk (D2): MEDIUM-HIGH
- KAPPA (ICML 2026) owns "knowledge vs prediction subspace + alignment fix" for MCQ; 2606.24952 owns "detection vs control angle"; 2605.05715 explicitly lists DAS/ReFT as next step with "our correct/incorrect supervision pairs provide the requisite training signal" — likely being done by that author. 2605.05115 authors list refusal/sycophancy as next targets. Open niche: per-item geometry comparison (failing vs aligned items) of the M_h→M_y map, at >8B, with aliasing controls.

### Kill criteria
- D2 KILL if: (a) on a task with item-level CRID (probe AUC ≥0.9 with mixed-context fitting), the rep-to-output map (geodesic/distance correlation, or principal angle between knowledge and prediction probes) shows no significant difference between act-against-CRID items and aligned items (TOST within a pre-set margin); OR (b) the best linear alternatives (KAPPA-style affine alignment, rotated direction) already close ≥80% of the gap, leaving nothing for nonlinear methods; OR (c) the CRID signal is explained by length/prompt-copy baselines within 3pp.
