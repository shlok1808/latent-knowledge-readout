# Direction 1a deep read: bypass vs inaccessible vs readout failure

Skill: `deep-research` (researcher reference). Sources were read through arxiv.org/html with WebFetch, which summarizes the page. Long pages were cut off at about 100k characters, so appendices are often unread; each entry says where. No PDFs were downloaded. Terms: "decodable" means a probe reads it out. "Causally controllable" means intervening on that direction changes behavior. "Endogenous mediation" means the unmodified model routes its decision through that direction.

## Q1. Priority 1 papers (KAPPA, Readout-Mediator Angle, Miscalibrated Readouts, Human Interventions as Labels)

### Takeaway
All four agree that "decodable when wrong" does not settle the fate. One paper (2609.04582) shows that much of what gets called hidden knowledge is already in the logit margin and fails only at the decision threshold, at least up to 8B. Two papers show directions that decode well but are causally inert and orthogonal to the true mediator (2605.29126 for dates, via DAS; 2610.21708 for ambiguity belief in agents). KAPPA shows the gap can be closed by a causal realignment, but only along linear k-dimensional subspaces.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. KAPPA: Bridging the Knowledge-Prediction Gap in LLMs on MCQs (2509.23782v4, Park, Pyun, Jo, SNU; ICML template, acceptance not confirmed on the page; sweep says ICML 2026)
- **(1) What:** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; generalization on Mistral-7B, Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-14B (>8B, TruthfulQA only); Qwen3-32B is mentioned with no numbers. MCQ: BBH, MMLU, ARC-C, TruthfulQA, GSM8k as MCQ, PubMedQA, BBQ-Age/Religion. — [arXiv 2509.23782](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782)
- **(5) Protocol:** residual stream at the final token of the answer prefix, every layer. Knowledge probe: k-class linear, trained on gold labels. Prediction probe: trained on the model's greedy answers. KAPPA is a closed-form minimal-ℓ2 affine edit that moves prediction-subspace coordinates to knowledge-subspace coordinates. Layers are chosen as the top-n consecutive by knowledge-probe accuracy among those where the prediction probe is above 85%. α=30 or 10, β=0, fixed across datasets. Applied at every decoding step. Baselines: CAA and DoLA. Controls: random subspaces (principal angle ≈88.7°, CKA ≲0.2), 10 distractor seeds, 10–80% training fractions, cross-dataset probe transfer. 32 prompt formats were scored to pick the most balanced. — [2509.23782](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782)
- **Numbers:** knowledge probe over base accuracy on TruthfulQA: +21.3 (Qwen) and +19.6 (Llama). BBQ-Age: +29.3 (Llama). Near zero on MMLU-SocSci and ARC-C. KAPPA on Llama TruthfulQA: 56.7→73.5; BBQ-Age 59.9→76.8. Free-form effect is small: Qwen TruthfulQA 41.7→44.2, GSM8k 91.6→90.7. Gap vs principal angle across benchmarks: ρ=0.976 for Llama (8 points); ρ=0.619, p=0.106 for Qwen, not significant. — [2509.23782](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782)
- **Readout check:** at intervention layers, the prediction subspace is about 65–70° from the answer-letter unembedding directions. The authors argue KAPPA does not work by simply shifting letter logits. — [2509.23782](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** consistent with an intermediate-layer misrouting between a knowledge subspace and a prediction subspace that can be repaired causally. That makes it inaccessible or misrouted rather than readout failure, and it is causal controllability, not shown endogenous mediation. There is no ablation of the knowledge subspace on wrong items, so it is not shown that the model ever routes through it. Knowledge is defined as linearly accessible, and nonlinear knowledge is explicitly out of scope. — [2509.23782](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23782)
- **(3) Gaps:** supervised (needs gold labels from the same distribution). The α/β sweep was run on TruthfulQA, which is also a test set. Only 8 benchmark points for the geometry link. GPT-4o-mini judge for free-form. No logit-margin baseline of the 2609.04582 kind, so part of the "gap" may be a threshold or position-bias effect in MCQ letters.
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "more reliably extracting knowledge from hidden states, aligning nonlinearly encoded knowledge"; also "extending to higher-dimensional interventions". Done by anyone? The quick check found no follow-up; 2609.04582 cites KAPPA as Park et al. (2025) but does not extend it.
- **(2) To check:** Orgad et al. 2025 (LLMs Know More Than They Show); Gekhman et al. 2025 (inside-outside knowledge gap); Marks & Tegmark 2024; Wiegreffe et al. 2025 (MCQ answer-selection mechanisms); Tulchinskii et al. 2024.
- **(7) Verdict: ADAPT.** Copy the paired knowledge-probe vs prediction-probe design and the random-subspace angle null. Add a margin baseline and an ablation of the knowledge subspace. Avoid tuning α on the test benchmark.
- **Single-direction flag:** the prediction subspace is k-dimensional and linear. Nonlinear and redundant routes are not ruled out.

#### 2. When and How Long? The Readout-Mediator Angle in Temporal Reasoning (2605.29126v1, Fadnavis, Kanakaraj, Wyss; 27 May 2026; no venue; missed by the sweeps)
- **(1) What:** calendar-date duration reasoning. Gemma-2-2B and Qwen2.5-1.5B as primary models; scale-up on Qwen2.5-7B and Gemma-2-9B (9B, just above 8B); Pythia-1.4B training checkpoints. Preliminary results on arithmetic and spatial displacement. — [arXiv 2605.29126](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29126), [html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29126)
- **(5) Protocol:** a sin/cos circular ridge probe for day-of-year at L⋆, chosen by bootstrap peak CV R² (L⋆=1 on Gemma-2-2B). Rank-matched DAS subspace (k=4), trained on task NLL. Ablation is projection-out, x − UᵀUx. Readout-mediator angle compared with a Haar random-subspace null; at least 25 random controls per measurement. A nine-method comparison on one specificity axis (probe, INLP, LEACE, gradient, PCA, attribution patching, DAS). Nonlinear probes. 240 non-calendar prompts for collateral damage. Data: a 3,650-example split; 2,020 attribution prompts; a 979-query v2 benchmark; a 75-query held-out set. The data license is not visible (the paper is CC BY-NC-ND). — [html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29126)
- **Numbers (Gemma-2-2B):** probe R²=0.981, yet probe ablation costs 0.6 pp, the same as random. DAS ablation takes accuracy from 42% to 0%; specificity ratio ρ₄≈1050. Probe-DAS angle ≈88°, against a Haar null of 88.3°. DAS drops are 42–51 pp across the four models; random controls are ≤0.3 pp. Arithmetic: probe 0.0 pp vs DAS 68 pp. Spatial: −6.0 vs 20.0 pp. SAE (GemmaScope): probe-aligned feature is "specific months" and DAS-aligned feature is "time/duration". Single-head ablation does nothing, but the top 10 heads together give −17.2 pp, with super-additive interactions. — [html](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29126)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** the strongest bypass result in this set. The probe direction is decodable, its ablation is inert, and the mediator is orthogonal at the random-null angle. It also tests some nonlinear routes: nonlinear probes give subspaces at the same null angle (Supp. S39, not read directly) and some redundant ones (head groups). The caveat: this is about an intermediate variable (date), not about correctness information while the model acts wrongly. The evidence is interventional (ablation, DAS); no analysis of natural variation, so not endogenous mediation in the strict sense.
- **(3) Gaps:** small models; the >8B claim stops at 9B. L⋆=1 is a very early layer. The other domains are preliminary. Cross-model transplants fail.
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "Whether the readout–mediator angle can guide the design of causally grounded monitors"; "ρk offers a principled check for when such monitors can be trusted." No follow-up found in the quick check.
- **(2) To check:** Geiger et al. DAS; Makelov et al. 2023 (2311.17030, interpretability illusion for subspace patching); Ravfogel INLP; Belrose LEACE; Elazar amnesic probing.
- **(7) Verdict: COPY.** The probe vs DAS rank-matched ablation, the Haar-null angle and the ρₖ specificity ratio are exactly the bypass test needed. Pair it with the Makelov illusion check, because DAS can find dormant pathways.

**Progress note:** written after papers 1–2.

#### 3. When Do Internal Probes Beat Reading the Answer? Miscalibrated Readouts and Behavior-Concealed Knowledge (2609.04582v1, Villuri, Shaik, Doboli, Stony Brook; 4 Sep 2026; no venue; CC BY 4.0)
- **(1) What:** Qwen3 0.6B, 1.7B, 4B and 8B instruct; 0.6B and 4B base; Phi-3.5-mini; SmolLM2-1.7B (behavior only). Logic verification (Zhou et al. 2026 corpus: 1,200 balanced trials plus 503 rule-generated foils), a maze task (1,600 hard, 1,300 easy), a generation audit, and a 120-configuration factorial. Nothing above 8B. License not stated. — [arXiv 2609.04582](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04582)
- **(5) Protocol:** ℓ2 logistic probes at the answer token and the last input token, leave-one-structure-out with nested layer CV (following Orgad et al. 2025). Cluster bootstrap with 2,000 resamples, and permutation tests. **Key baseline: the YES−NO logit margin read with no training.** Controls: lexical overlap, a frozen 768-d text encoder, candidate-only and premise-shuffled probes, cross-family probing, and a 10-feature answer-surface baseline. Repairs: one fitted threshold τ, median-centering, Platt scaling, content-free calibration (fails, 55.9%), and few-shot examples. — [2609.04582](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04582)
- **Numbers:** Qwen3-0.6B behavior 0.500, margin AUC 0.887, probe AUC 0.964, calibrated 0.810. Qwen3-4B: 0.932, 0.979, 0.989. Qwen3-8B: free-form 0.848, forced choice 0.943, margin 0.980, probe 0.989. The probe−margin gap shrinks from +0.076 to about +0.01. Across 90 configurations, accuracy tracks threshold offset (Spearman −0.93). Few-shot examples move the offset from +4.64σ to +0.01σ while margin ranking is preserved (ρ=0.79). At 8B, 113 of 1,200 free-form responses contain neither answer token, so the failure is in the answer channel. In the generation setting, a 10-feature surface baseline matches or beats hidden-state probes under some label schemes. — [2609.04582](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04582)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** strong evidence for readout or threshold failure, not bypass, in forced-choice verification. The information survives to the logits and only the decision threshold is wrong. The authors' caveat: "Probes remain correlational: we do not causally steer the threshold at the activation level." — [2609.04582](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04582)
- **(3) Gaps:** synthetic and English-only; ≤8B; part of the logic signal is candidate-intrinsic; LLM-generated foils; "whether the ladder's ordering holds on natural, noisy-label tasks is untested."
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "we leave its mechanism open" (extended reasoning); "its fine-tuning dynamics remain open". No follow-up found.
- **(2) To check:** Orgad et al. 2025; Gekhman et al. 2025; Zhao et al. 2021 (calibrate before use); KAPPA.
- **(7) Verdict: COPY as the mandatory first control.** Any bypass claim must first beat the logit margin and a fitted threshold, as an "evidence ladder": margin → calibrated margin → probe → probe minus candidate-only.
- **Single-direction flag:** not applicable; there is no causal test.

#### 4. Human Interventions as Labels: Localizing, Steering, and (Sometimes) Editing the Computations Behind LLM Agent Failures (2610.21708v1, Zavattari, Tommasi, Prencipe, Pisa; 15 Oct 2026; no venue)
- **(1) What:** Qwen2.5-3B, gemma-2-2b, OLMo-3-7B, Mistral-Nemo-12B (>8B) and Phi-4 14B (>8B). Five of 13 families were eligible. A synthetic "clarify" testbed where the agent outputs ACTION or ASK, using twin pairs that differ only in workspace ambiguity: 300 pairs per seed, 3 seeds. Labels come from a deterministic rule overseer ("rule-v2"), agreeing with humans at κ≈0.90–0.97. Not real human logs. Code is on GitHub with a Zenodo snapshot; license not seen. — [arXiv 2610.21708](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21708)
- **(5) Protocol:** logistic probes at the last prompt token for each layer. A behavior probe (will it ask?) and a belief probe (is the request ambiguous?). Controls: permuted-label canaries, a positional canary, and a bag-of-words content baseline, which itself reaches 0.93–0.99, so decodability alone is not a finding. Steering at the causal site with a random direction of the same norm. A "style" direction (ALWAYS ask vs NEVER ask system prompts). Belief-axis patching in both directions. SAE feature clamping on gemma L21. A LoRA confined to the causal band vs behavior cloning. — [2610.21708](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21708)
- **Numbers:** behavior-direction flips at an extrapolated dose: Qwen 97%, gemma 83%, OLMo 72%, Nemo-12B 95%, Phi-4 100%. Random directions flip 0, with a maximum of 1/74 anywhere. At the class-mean dose, flips are only 12–20% on four families. **The belief direction is decodable but causally inert in both directions and orthogonal to the decision axis (cos≈0)**: the authors' "H-caution" (the request is known to be clear, yet the agent asks). Phi-4 is the exception ("H-misrep"): its over-asks track genuine internal uncertainty. A system-prompt instruction made over-asking worse. — [2610.21708](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21708)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** bypass of correctness-relevant belief in four of five families, tested at >8B (Nemo-12B). The behavior direction is causally controllable, but the paper does not show endogenous mediation. The SAE caveat is one dictionary, one layer, one dose. "Belief inert" rests on a single probe direction plus one SAE; redundant and nonlinear routes are not ruled out.
- **(3) Gaps:** synthetic and greedy-only; when localized editing beats imitation is "the paper's most important open question", and two pre-written geometric rules were falsified.
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "naturalistic replication is future work"; "deploying the same pipeline on naturally occurring human logs is the obvious next step"; "sampled-decoding replications are cheap and pending". Not done yet; the paper is one week old.
- **(2) To check:** Cheng et al. 2026 (= 2605.14038); Wang et al. 2026 "Lazy Agent"/ASA; Min et al. 2025; ClarifySAE (Petrova & Kovalev 2026); Orgad et al. 2026 (actionable interpretability); Bugaud 2026 (predict-intervene-validate).
- **(7) Verdict: COPY** the twin-pair design, belief vs behavior probes, both-sign belief patching, the content-baseline canary and the H-caution / H-misrep split. **Scoop risk is high** for an agentic bypass-vs-override project; see the synthesis.

**Progress note:** written after papers 3–4.

### Inferences
- A three-way test is needed: (a) does the information survive into the logit margin (2609.04582)? (b) If not, is the probe direction inert while a rank-matched DAS subspace is causal (2605.29126)? (c) Is the belief direction inert in both signs while the decision direction is causal (2610.21708)? Only (b) speaks directly to redundant routes.
- None of the four shows endogenous mediation, meaning natural trial-by-trial variation in the mediator predicting behavior once interventions are controlled for.

### Gaps
- KAPPA appendix (dataset sizes and licenses) not read; truncated at 100k characters.
- 2605.29126 Supp. S39 (nonlinear probes) was seen only in summary.

## Q2. Priority 2 papers

### Takeaway
The priority-2 set divides by locus. **Late bottleneck or override:** 2609.37737 (attack info decodable from layer 1, causal leverage only at 63–91% depth). **Recognized but not routed:** 2608.29109 (cos≈0.09 to refusal, up to 70B, predates instruction tuning), 2605.14038 (correlational only). **Readout or selection failure:** 2606.16364 (gold tool attended, 59% fixed by output-logit bias alone, 91% by attention or residual levers). **Rerouted, as a measured regime:** 2610.05292 (SFT keeps R=0.98 but U=0.15). Two papers are weaker than their titles: 2609.02438 has no margin baseline and behavior at chance through label bias, and 2605.03258's row-only fix fails in generation.

### Cited Findings

#### 5. Where Do LLMs Decide to Break the Rules? Mechanistic Localization of Prompt Injection Compliance (2609.37737v1, Wen, Liu, Yang, Sakuma, Sun; RIKEN AIP; cs.CR; 29 Sep 2026; CC BY 4.0)
- **(1) What:** Qwen3-4B as primary model; Qwen3-14B, Qwen3-32B (>8B), Phi-3-mini, Mistral-7B; also Qwen3-4B-Base and Qwen3-30B-A3B MoE. A synthetic "AcmeCorp" role-conflict set (300 test queries) plus deepset, neuralchemy, BIPIA, Gandalf, InjecAgent (62 successful attacks) and AgentDojo (24 successful attacks). — [arXiv 2609.37737](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37737)
- **(5) Protocol:** at the last prompt token, replace the compliant-run state with the mean resistant state at each layer, and measure the flip rate. Rank-k PCA subspace patching. LinearSVC probes on 128 PCA components. Sublayer split into attention-only, MLP-only and full residual. Controls: a rank-8 random subspace, a content-matched donor, the compliant centroid, norm-matched noise, and a benign mean. Labels come from keyword matching validated by an LLM judge. — [2609.37737](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37737)
- **Numbers:** attack-presence probes are ≥80% at every layer, yet flips are negligible through L18. The peak is at 63–91% depth (Qwen3-4B L24: 91.7%; 14B: 85.5%; Phi-3-mini: 77.1%). Rank 8 suffices for 4B and 14B; 32B needs rank 64. The random rank-8 subspace gives 38.1% vs 85.7% for the learned one. At L24, attention-only and MLP-only patches give 21.4% each vs 90.5% for the full residual, so L24 is a "leverage point", not where the decision is computed. Naturalistic benchmarks give about 12%; BIPIA and Gandalf give 66–70%. Reasoning mode peaks at 0.21 and MoE at 0.06, near random. — [2609.37737](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37737)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** early decodability without early causal leverage, with a late bottleneck. This fits a late override or decision point, and the early signal is either bypassed or not yet integrated. The patching is mean-centroid replacement, which tests causal controllability, not endogenous mediation. "Not used early" rests on mean-patching failure, which can miss redundant routes (patching one layer while later layers recompute).
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "Localizing the distributed agentic decision (e.g. multi-token or multi-step patching) is future work." Not done as far as the quick check shows.
- **(2) To check:** Arditi et al. 2024; Wen et al. 2025 (instruction detection); Geiger et al. 2025 (causal abstraction).
- **(7) Verdict: ADAPT** the layer-sweep flip-rate curve against the probe-accuracy curve (the "decodable-early, causal-late" plot), plus the content-matched donor control. Avoid reading single-token localization as the agentic locus; it fails in agentic settings.

#### 6. Recognition-Refusal Misalignment in LLMs: Why Models Answer Structurally Unanswerable Questions (2608.29109v1, Du (USC), Hu (ASU); 29 Aug 2026; sweep says EMNLP 2026, not confirmed on the page)
- **(1) What:** 11 instruct models from 1.7B to 70B: SmolLM2-1.7B, Phi-4-mini, Mistral-7B, Mistral-Small-24B, Gemma-3-4B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.3-70B, Qwen3-8B/14B/32B, OLMo-2-13B, plus base/instruct pairs. Datasets: math800 and code800 (1,600 prompts each, LLM-generated, MIT); fact800 (SQuAD 2.0, CC BY-SA 4.0); FalseQA (1,374, not redistributed); an AbstentionBench GSM8K subset (CC BY-NC 4.0). — [arXiv 2608.29109](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.29109)
- **(5) Protocol:** the recognition direction is a 1-D "A-null" MeanDiff: PCA with k=100 on answerable states is projected out, then scored by cosine at a matched layer, over 5 seeds. The refusal direction follows Arditi (50 harmful, 50 harmless). Steering adds α·d̂ with α∈{5,10,20,40}σ in both signs, with random-direction controls and a "gated flip rate". Refusal-only scoring checks the effect is not refusal vocabulary. Controls for length (length-only AUC 0.500), difficulty, category and scale. — [2608.29109](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.29109)
- **Numbers:** recognition AUC 0.939 averaged over 22 cells (0.841–0.993). Cosine between recognition and refusal: mean 0.087 (0.020–0.130), with CIs excluding alignment. Full-space cosine 0.240, mostly from shared answerable-structure variance. A behavior-defined invalidity direction has cos 0.40 with recognition and 0.08 with refusal. Steering gives +33 to +44 pp on Mistral-7B; the effects are weaker and asymmetric elsewhere. Base models already show the low-cosine geometry (base recognition AUC ≈0.98; Δcos instruct−base mean +0.037; Llama-70B ≈0), so **the misalignment predates instruction tuning**. — [2608.29109](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.29109)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** the recognition information is present and causally controllable when injected, but is not read by the refusal pathway. That is routing, not encoding: either bypass or inaccessible to the refusal readout. Caveats: no projection-ablation on unmodified generation; single directions are the main analysis (a 5–10-dimensional subspace check on 4 cells agrees); nonlinear routes are open; labels are LLM-assisted.
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "future work could train the safety-refusal pathway to read"; "direct structural-impossibility supervision may be needed". Not checked in time.
- **(2) To check:** Arditi 2024; Ma et al. 2026 (unreasonable math problems); Wu et al. 2026 (recognition vs execution axes in refusal); Wollschläger et al. 2025 (concept cones).
- **(7) Verdict: COPY** the A-null MeanDiff (it removes shared task variance before taking the cosine; full-space cosine inflates alignment) and the refusal-vocabulary-only scoring control. This is the only >8B (to 70B) test here of a "recognized but not acted on" geometry.

#### 7. When Decodability Is Not Enough: Logical Validity Representations, Behavioral Dissociation, and Causal Tests (2609.02438v1, Muthya Sudheendra & Srivastava, U Minnesota; no venue)
- **(1) What:** Pythia-1.4B, Pythia-2.8B, SmolLM3-3B, Llama-3.2-3B, Mistral-7B (all ≤8B, quantized). 800 synthetic items in 400 matched valid/invalid pairs; license not stated. — [arXiv 2609.02438](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02438)
- **(5) Protocol:** ℓ2 logistic probe at the final prompt token for every block, with layer chosen by validation AUROC. Held out by template, domain and family. Steering only (no ablation): α∈{±1,2,4}×SD along the probe direction, against 5 norm-matched random directions, plus matched-projection patching. — [2609.02438](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02438)
- **Numbers:** behavior is at chance (0.47–0.50) with strong label bias (SmolLM3 and Mistral answer VALID throughout). The probe reaches AUROC ≈0.99–1.00 on the behaviorally incorrect subset (Llama-3.2). At α=+4, ΔM is ±0.002–0.004 with no flips, smaller than random directions (|ΔM| 0.006–0.008). — [2609.02438](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02438)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** looks like bypass, **but the setup is exactly the one 2609.04582 shows is often a threshold or label-bias failure**: behavior at 0.500 from always answering VALID. No logit-margin baseline is reported, so readout failure is not ruled out. It uses a single linear direction with no nonlinear or DAS tests. The authors' own caveat: "Weak steering effects don't show validity is causally irrelevant."
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "Replication with larger models and a broader range of architectures would help establish how general these patterns are."
- **(2) To check:** Bertolazzi et al. 2026 (validity vs plausibility in syllogisms); Sahoo et al. 2026 (probes detect format).
- **(7) Verdict: AVOID as evidence for bypass; ADAPT** the error-conditioned held-out AUROC split. Rerun it with a margin baseline and DAS before citing it.

#### 8. Model-Adaptive Tool Necessity Reveals the Knowing-Doing Gap in LLM Tool Use (2605.14038v2, Cheng, Fan, JafariRaviz, Rezaei, Feizi, UMD; sweep D says NeurIPS 2026 poster, not on the arXiv page; CC BY 4.0)
- **(1) What:** Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-4B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Llama-3.2-3B (none above 8B). Arithmetic with a calculator (4,000 synthetic items) and TruthfulQA with search (817). Data license not stated. — [arXiv 2605.14038](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14038)
- **(5) Protocol:** **Model-adaptive necessity label:** 10 tool-free samples at T=0.7; any failure means the tool is necessary. Linear probes at every layer × last 20 query tokens, scored by MCC on a 30% held-out split. A cognition (necessity) probe and an action (tool-call) probe; cosine between their directions per (layer, token). — [2605.14038](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14038)
- **Numbers:** end-to-end mismatch is 26.5–54.0% on arithmetic and 30.8–41.8% on TruthfulQA. Most mismatch is in the cognition→action step, and it is not concentrated where cognition confidence is near 0.5. Directions are moderately aligned at mid positions and near-orthogonal at late layers of the final token. Self-assessment prompts change tool behavior on up to about 50% of samples. — [2605.14038](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14038)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** correlational only (no steering). Consistent with bypass or misrouting at the action readout, but it cannot tell bypass from override. Orthogonality of two probe directions does not show non-use; 2605.29126 shows probe directions can be orthogonal to the true mediator anyway.
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "It could be beneficial to cover other instantiations of this definition with different N, T values." 2610.21708 effectively adds the causal step in a related ask/act setting.
- **(2) To check:** Wang et al. 2026 (tools only when epistemically necessary; ASA); Li et al. 2025 (meta-cognition trigger).
- **(7) Verdict: COPY the model-adaptive label.** Correctness relative to this model's own sampled ability is the right label for "it knew". AVOID treating probe-cosine as evidence of fate.

#### 9. Erased, Rerouted, or Rescaled? Post-Training and the Causal Quotient of a Language Model's Belief State (2610.05292v1, Li, Zheng, Wu, Chen; 4 Oct 2026; no venue)
- **(1) What:** small 4-block transformers and GRUs on hidden Markov worlds with exact belief states. Base/post-trained pairs of Qwen3-1.7B, Qwen3-4B and Qwen3.5-4B, plus LoRA GRPO on the bases. Nothing above 8B. — [arXiv 2610.05292](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05292)
- **(5) Protocol, the cleanest operational fate taxonomy found:** **Erased** = represented R falls. **Rerouted** = R stays but use U falls. **Rescaled** = R and U stay but the variance share A falls. R is a whitened least-squares probe R², invariant under random invertible maps. U is the decision difference between "twin" histories (same task symbols, different nuisance symbols), **measured without a probe**. A is the variance share plus RankMe. Extra checks: interchange interventions, a probe frozen at base vs refit (detects re-encoding), and steps needed to train a fresh readout (practical accessibility). — [2610.05292](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05292)
- **Numbers:** at matched reward, SFT reroutes (R_vis=0.98, U=0.15). KL-anchored RL/GRPO/DPO keep within-class slope γ at 0.93–1.02. Erasure appears only under strong weight decay. A GRU stops using the invisible component (about 0.01 of base) while half stays decodable. Rescaling changes variance share 20–100× with no change in R² or interchange effect, yet slows fresh-readout recovery. In the LMs, post-training changes peak belief R² by ≤0.05 while late-layer effective rank drops 12–19%. — [2610.05292](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05292)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** direct evidence that "decodable but unused" (rerouted) is a real, distinct regime induced by SFT-like objectives. It also shows "shrunk" (rescaled) can be invisible to both probe R² and interchange tests but still matters for accessibility. It goes beyond a single probe through twin-history U and interchange tests. Caveats: exact only in synthetic HMMs; only one LM family; linear probes only.
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "moves the quotient or only the support of the belief state remains to be studied."; "What makes amplification the cheaper direction in a pretrained language model is open."
- **(7) Verdict: COPY the R/U/A decomposition.** The twin-history use test is the best available probe-free operationalization of "used", and it is closer to endogenous mediation than steering is. Adapt it to correctness: twins that differ only in the correctness-relevant cue.

#### 10. The Right Answer, the Wrong Direction: Why Transformers Fail at Counting and How to Fix It (2605.03258v2, Gabriel Garcia, independent; 15 May 2026; no venue)
- **(1) What:** Qwen3-8B as main model, Mistral-7B, Pythia-410M/160M, Qwen3-14B (>8B). Synthetic cat-counting prompts (4,320, full factorial) and NL counting. — [arXiv 2605.03258](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03258)
- **(5) Protocol:** ridge probes at the entity-mean and last-token positions (R²≈0.99; 0.997 at L3). Cosine of count directions against the digit unembedding rows. Fixes: retraining the 9 digit rows of lm_head (36,864 parameters); rank-16 Q/V LoRA; "DPS" probe-count injection into the logits. Controls: shuffled row assignment, random-direction DPS, shuffled-label probe (R²≈−0.04), permutation p=0.79 on alignment, MMLU/GSM8K negative controls. — [2605.03258](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03258)
- **Numbers:** |cos| ≤0.032 against a random ≈0.013 baseline, so alignment is at chance. Row repair gives 60.7–100% under constrained decoding **but 0% greedy generation**. LoRA reaches 83.1% ±7.2. Hard DPS reaches 72.4%. LoRA leaves the early probe direction unchanged (|cos| 0.0089→0.0070), so the fix acts on later routing. Logit-lens evidence is mixed across seeds. — [2605.03258](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03258)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** the title claims readout failure, but the evidence leans toward **inaccessible or misrouted** rather than pure unembedding failure. The row-only fix fails in free generation, and the working fix rewires attention routing. "Orthogonal to unembedding rows" is weak by itself: intermediate-layer directions need not align with unembedding rows (the 2605.29126 lesson). Single author, and the summary itself is internally mixed.
- **(7) Verdict: ADAPT** the readout-fix ladder (row-only → injection → routing LoRA) as a diagnostic for where the failure sits. Avoid the cosine-to-unembedding argument as the main evidence.

#### 11. Looking Is Not Picking: An Attention-Segment Account of Tool-Selection Failures in LLM Agents (2606.16364v2, Shiyang Chen, BIT; AAAI-format, venue not confirmed)
- **(1) What:** Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5 0.5B–14B, Qwen2.5-32B (>8B), Yi-1.5-9B, Qwen3-8B, xLAM-2-8B, and others. A synthetic 24-tool library; BFCL live_multiple (300 tasks per model; 900 tasks across 3 models with 198 failures); Seal-Tools (294); τ-bench airline (120 users, teacher-forced). — [arXiv 2606.16364](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.16364)
- **(5) Protocol:** per-segment attention mass from the decision tokens to each tool definition. The margin is gold minus mean-distractor attention, with sink mass logged. The causal lever is an additive attention-logit bias δ to gold keys at all layers and heads. Constrained scoring by teacher-forced name log-probability. Controls: description-only boost, shuffled-label probe (AUROC ≈0.5), name-token de-circularization, held-out τ and δ. — [2606.16364](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.16364)
- **Numbers:** in 80% of real failures, gold is the most-attended tool (chance 21%), and it out-attends the chosen tool in about 90%. Recovery: attention boost 90.9% (2.7% broken), oracle residual steering 90.9% (1.9%), output-logit bias 59.1% (0%), description-only boost 43.9% (38.7% broken), prompt reorder 18.2%, duplication 16.7%. Attention and residual levers fix the same items (Jaccard 0.87). — [2606.16364](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.16364)
- **(6) Fate evidence:** information is attended and present, but the decision readout fails. This is **readout or selection failure, not erasure**. A partial logit fix (59%) recovers part, so some failure sits upstream of the final logits. Caveats: the constrained protocol loses about 19 points to free generation on AST, which confounds the result; the margin is partly circular; single-seed correlational runs; no full agent loop. Attention mass is a correlational "looking" measure; whether the model naturally routes value from that segment is untested.
- **(4) Next steps (quoted):** "a full agent loop is future work". Multi-turn transfer is "a sharp, honest open problem".
- **(7) Verdict: ADAPT** the lever comparison (prompt vs output-logit vs attention vs residual) as a locus test. The share recovered by an output-logit bias is a cheap upper bound on "pure readout failure".

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings

#### 12. Decoded but Unused: Instruction Tuning Routes Moral Framing into the Judgment Readout (OpenReview da9ylT2doy; Konrad, Tanyel, Ayvaz; **Mech Interp Workshop @ ICML 2026 poster**, published 11 Jun 2026; CC BY 4.0). Read via Chrome, abstract and TL;DR only (no PDF opened).
- Moral framing is "already linearly decodable in the pretrained network yet has no causal effect on its judgment". In the instruction-tuned checkpoint, the same representation becomes aligned with and causally usable by the evaluative readout. Framing-judgment alignment is 8.4× larger than in the matched base at the same layer. TL;DR: instruction tuning "routes an existing framing signal into the model's evaluative readout through a localized mid-network pathway." — [OpenReview da9ylT2doy](https://openreview.net/forum?id=da9ylT2doy)
- Keywords list probes, activation patching and residual-stream analysis. Model names and sizes were not visible in the abstract.
- **Fate:** bypass in base, use after instruction tuning. This is "transformed in readout, not in representation". Workshop paper, abstract-only read, so strength is weak to moderate.
- **Verdict: ADAPT** the matched base-vs-instruct checkpoint at the same layer as a natural experiment for whether a readout reads a variable. Check the PDF for models and whether "no causal effect" is a one-direction test.

#### 13. Few-Shot Demonstrations Elicit the Use of In-Context World Representations in LLMs (2609.24352v1; Matsutani, Minegishi, Park, Kojima, Iwasawa, Matsuo; 21 Sep 2026; no venue). Abstract only.
- Graph tracking, ARC-AGI-1/2, web-agent tasks, Othello; 6 models from 4 families (sizes not on the abstract page). "few-shot demonstrations relocate the world representation and increase its predictive use". The shifted representation is nearly orthogonal to the original subspace. Intervening on it hurts performance more selectively than other subspaces do. — [arXiv 2609.24352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24352)
- **Fate:** a prompt-induced move from present-but-less-used to used, via relocation. That is "transformed", which warns that a probe trained in one condition may miss the relocated representation. No zero-shot decodable-but-unused number on the abstract page.
- **Verdict: ADAPT** few-shot vs zero-shot as a within-model manipulation of use. This connects to 2609.04582, where few-shot fixed the threshold.

#### 14. Matryoshka Attribution: Learning to attribute LM outputs to representations and weights (2609.25518v1; Arora, Acharya, Hu, Zhang, Goodman, Jurafsky, Potts; 22 Sep 2026; CC BY 4.0). Abstract only.
- A learned nested mask (sigmoid top-k with random k) that orders components by attribution at every sparsity. It is first on the MIB leaderboard. Restoring 1% of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct's weights to base removes refusals while keeping capabilities. — [arXiv 2609.25518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25518)
- **As a tool:** it gives a causal, loss-based criterion over components, not directions, so it complements DAS. It could localize which components carry the correctness signal into the decision, and the weight-attribution mode could find which fine-tuning weight changes created a bypass. **Verdict: ADAPT** as a localization tool. It does not test whether a probe direction is used.

#### 15. LLMs Know the Constraint But Do Not Use It: Activation Bottlenecks in Pragmatic Constraint Reasoning (2608.12321v1; Li, Krishnan, Padman; 29 May 2026). Abstract only. BELONGS HERE.
- A Knowledge / Symmetry / Routing / Repair "quartet diagnostic" over 14 models. Probes on two open-weight models decode the constraint at >88%. Patching repairs one model (+6.4 nats) and not the other (−0.07). Prompted interventions only raise conservative bias, through a "prerequisite mention" mediator. "Hidden-constraint failure is a routing problem, not a knowledge problem." — [arXiv 2608.12321](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12321)
- **Fate:** routing or bypass in one model; in the other, the failure is not repairable by donor patching (inaccessible, or redundant routes). **Verdict: COPY the quartet**, especially the symmetry check that the encoding is the same on constraint-present and constraint-absent prompts, before making a routing claim.

#### 16. Knowledge Knows, Verbalization Tells: Disentangling Latent Directions for Mathematical Solvability (2607.05013v1; Xiros, Zoumpoulidi, Paraskevopoulos; 6 Jul 2026; 14 pp). Abstract only. BELONGS HERE.
- Solvability knowledge and verbalization are distinct linearly decodable representations. Fabrication is "primarily associated with changes in verbalization rather than the underlying knowledge". Unsolvability cues act through verbalization. Steering improves abstention. — [arXiv 2607.05013](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05013)
- **Fate:** knowledge intact, failure on a separate verbalization variable. That fits readout failure or bypass, parallel to 2608.29109 (recognition vs refusal). Models and sizes were not on the abstract page.

#### 17. Simulated Adoption: Decoupling Magnitude and Direction in LLM In-Context Conflict Resolution (2602.04918v2; Zhang & Lin; Feb 2026). Abstract only. PERIPHERAL.
- Qwen-3-4B, Llama-3.1-8B, GLM-4-9B (9B). Counterfactual context adds a quasi-orthogonal vector that rotates the hidden state away from the truth unembedding; norm is stable in 2 of 3 architectures. — [arXiv 2602.04918](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04918)
- **Fate:** "transformed" (rotated) or overridden, not erased. Purely observational geometry with no causal test on the abstract page. Weak evidence, but it supplies a magnitude-vs-angle decomposition to copy as a cheap descriptive measure.

#### 18. The Agent Knew: Internal States Forecast Unauthorized Actions and Stop Them, and the Lies That Follow (2610.17301v1; Fayyazi, Kamal, Pedram; 12 Oct 2026; preregistered). Abstract only. PERIPHERAL (monitoring).
- CAPE-Bench (matched tasks where one fact decides authorization), τ²-bench, AgentDojo; Gemma-4 and gpt-oss ("frontier open-weight", likely >8B, sizes not on the page). A linear residual readout forecasts violations before the action (AUROC 0.94). Gating cuts Gemma-4 violations from 0.70 to 0.09. AgentDojo attack success falls from 11.7% to 5.7% (Gemma-4) and from 28.5% to 17.5% (gpt-oss). — [arXiv 2610.17301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17301)
- **Fate:** not tested. It shows the violation is decodable before acting, not that authorization info is bypassed. Useful as a CAPE-Bench data source with one-fact matched pairs at large scale. A forecast of the action is a behavior probe, not a belief probe, so it does not bear on the 2610.21708 split.

## Q5. Strand synthesis

### Takeaway
The evidence supports three distinct, co-existing fates, and which one applies is task- and model-specific. **Readout or threshold failure** dominates simple forced-choice verification at ≤8B (2609.04582, 2606.16364). **Bypass**, meaning decodable, inert along the probe direction, with an orthogonal mediator, is shown cleanly only for intermediate variables (2605.29126) and agent ambiguity belief (2610.21708), and as a trainable state (2610.05292, da9ylT2doy). **Late override or bottleneck** appears in injection compliance (2609.37737). No paper establishes endogenous mediation for any of these. All causal evidence is interventional controllability.

### Scoop risk (bypass-vs-override project)
- **High in the agentic ask/act niche:** 2610.21708 (15 Oct 2026) already runs belief vs behavior probes, both-sign belief patching, random controls and an H-caution (bypass-like) vs H-misrep split at up to 14B. 2605.14038 has the tool-necessity knowing-doing gap.
- **High in methodology:** "decodability ≠ use" has 2605.29126, 2609.31401 plus "Lagged Coupling", "Decodability Is Not Use" (ICLR 2027, desk rejected), "Decodability is Not Causality" (COLM WAB), "Readable ≠ Steerable", and "A Probe Direction Is a Property of Its Prompt".
- **Open niche:** a single paradigm that sorts each failure into readout (margin or threshold), bypass (probe inert, DAS mediator, twin-history U≈0), inaccessible (no repair by donor patching or DAS), or override (late suppression of a used signal). It would run on correctness information at act time, across >8B models, and test endogenous mediation, not only steering. No paper combines the 2609.04582 margin ladder with the 2605.29126 DAS angle and the 2610.05292 R/U/A.

### Candidate open questions and smallest first experiments
1. **Do the "bypass" results survive the margin control?** Smallest experiment: rerun 2609.02438's 800-item validity set (or 2608.29109's math800) on Qwen3-8B. Compute probe AUC vs YES−NO margin AUC vs fitted-threshold accuracy on wrong items. If the margin carries the signal, the failure is a readout failure and not bypass.
2. **Is the correctness probe direction the mediator?** Smallest experiment: on wrong-answer items in one task (e.g. 2610.21708's clarify testbed, whose code is public), fit a rank-4 DAS subspace for the decision. Measure the probe-DAS angle against a Haar null and ablation specificity ρₖ. Bypass needs probe ablation ≈ random and a DAS angle ≈ null.
3. **Endogenous mediation:** does natural trial-to-trial variance in the correctness subspace predict behavior after conditioning on the input (twin-history U, from 2610.05292)? Smallest experiment: twin prompts differing only in the correctness cue; measure the change in decision with no intervention, and compare with the change in the projection.
4. **Bypass vs override at act time:** does a late layer actively suppress a signal that an earlier readout uses (override), or does it never read it (bypass)? Smallest experiment: a layer sweep of flip rate (2609.37737 protocol) together with a sweep of probe accuracy and DAS-projection norm on the same items. Override predicts that the mediator projection drops after the bottleneck; bypass predicts the mediator never carries it.
5. **Does post-training create bypass?** Smallest experiment: matched base vs instruct (da9ylT2doy design) on 2608.29109's math800. Their base-instruct Δcos≈0 suggests not for recognition; test U, not cosine.
