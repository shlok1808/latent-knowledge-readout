# How Do Linear Probes Emerge? A Circuit-Tracing Framework with Concept-Targeted Attribution

Palit, Draye, Zhang, Schoelkopf, Jin. arXiv 2608.27510v1, 27 Aug 2026. Slug: `u5-user-2608.27510`. Shlok sent this one in, unsure whether it is relevant.

## Verdict first
- **Relevance: moderate as a method, weak as evidence.** The headline claim, that "knowing and saying" use separable circuits, is close to our **bypass** fate. But it is shown at 1-2B, on classification sentences where the next token is not a decision about the concept, and without size-matched random-ablation controls.
- **Most useful fact for us:** truthfulness is the exception. Its probe signal is tangled up with generation through *shared* nodes:
  - ablating the shared nodes drops the probe score 93% and flips the token in 67% of prompts;
  - even ablating nodes found only in the probe circuit flips 25%.

  This is a prior that factual-correctness information is less likely to be cleanly bypassed than toxicity or sentiment.
- **Reuse: adapt** the two-outcome, three-subset ablation design. **Avoid** the graph-topology "probe quality" predictor and the belief-point definition as written.
- **Blocker:** the method needs cross-layer transcoders (CLTs). The paper uses open CLTs for Gemma-2-2B and Llama-3.2-1B only. It says nothing about 9-27B.

## Setup
- **Models:** Gemma-2-2B (main; 26 layers, d_model 2304) and Llama-3.2-1B (appendix; 16 layers).
- **Data:** four binary concepts, each with 5000 examples per class, inputs of 60 tokens or fewer, an 80/20 split and seed 42.
  - Toxicity: ParaDetox toxic vs detoxified pairs.
  - Sentiment: SST-2.
  - Reasoning: HotpotQA multi-hop vs TriviaQA single-hop.
  - Truthfulness: Geometry of Truth (Counterfactuals, Cities and Spanish-English translations pooled).
  - Graph analyses use 100 prompts per concept per layer. The causal ablations use 50 prompts per concept.
- **Readout:** last-token residual stream.
  - Probe: one difference-in-means direction per layer, used as the attribution target.
  - Accuracy: measured with an LDA-corrected direction in a PCA-200 subspace and a midpoint threshold.
  - The concept directions are Gram-Schmidt orthogonalised.
- **Belief layer Lb** = the layer with the steepest single-step rise in mean positive-class probe score.
  - Gemma: Toxicity L22, Sentiment L21, Reasoning L15, Truthfulness L14.
  - Llama: Toxicity L14, Sentiment L15, Reasoning L9, Truthfulness L1.
- **Attribution:** open CLTs from Circuit-Tracer, loaded via CLT-Forge.
  - Edges come from a Jacobian with attention, norms and MLP nonlinearities frozen.
  - Graphs are pruned to nodes covering 80% of the effect and edges covering 95%.
  - **CTA** is the graph rooted at the probe score. **LTA** is the graph rooted at the next-token logit.
- **Analyses:**
  1. Ablate probe-exclusive, logit-exclusive and shared nodes. Measure ΔS (change in probe score) and the top-1 flip rate.
  2. 35 graph statistics predict probe accuracy (Gradient Boosting and Ridge, 5-fold group-aware CV, layer baseline, de-meaned target, permutation and bootstrap).
  3. Binary feature presence predicts the probe margin (Ridge).
  4. Presence enrichment of the top features vs a random feature.
  5. Jaccard overlap across adjacent layers and across concepts.
- **Controls missing:**
  - a size-matched random ablation;
  - a stated ablation method (zero, mean or replacement);
  - a concept-relevant behavioural outcome;
  - seeds or checkpoints;
  - any model above 2B.
- **Compute:** one A100 80 GB. About 10,400 graphs for Gemma. The feature predictor took about 26 h, the structural predictor 5 h, the ablations 3 h and probe training 3 h.

## Key results, with quotes
- **Double dissociation:** "Removing probe-relevant features reduces internal concept scores while largely preserving generated tokens, whereas removing logit-relevant features changes the generated token in 92% to 100% of cases" (p1).
- **Truthfulness is the exception:** "The dissociation is weaker for Truthfulness. In this case, probe-exclusive ablation produces a smaller change in concept score, and the shared subgraph carries greater dependence." (p5)
  - "For Truthfulness, ablating the shared subgraph causes a 93% drop in probe score (∆S = −38.94 from a baseline of +41.87) with a 67% flip rate" (p19).
  - "The probe-exclusive subgraph produces only a 25% flip rate, unlike the strict 0% seen for Toxicity and Sentiment, suggesting partial routing of the truthfulness representation" (p19).
- **Size confound:** "The logit-exclusive subgraph is 1.77–4.15× larger than the probe-exclusive subgraph across concepts" (p19).
- **Llama replication:** probe-exclusive ablation "produces a measurable internal effect on the probe score while causing 0% top-1 prediction flips across all concepts" (p24).
- **Structural predictor:** "graph-level features predict probe accuracy across four widely studied concept categories (ρ = 0.91, R2 = 0.84)" (p1). "GB exceeds it by ∆ρ = 0.83 on the target, confirming the signal is not simply a within-concept layer trend" (p17).
- **It does not hold up in Llama:**
  - "the withinconcept (de-meaned) signal is substantially weaker: ρ = +0.34 with a bootstrap CI that straddles zero [−0.055, +0.743]" (p24);
  - "Negative d values indicate that lower-accuracy layers exhibit higher feature values, a pattern inverted relative to Gemma-2-2B." (p29);
  - "Concept-transfer is also poor across the board: held-out concept ρ ranges from −0.74 to +0.48 for the raw target" (p25).
- **Distributed concepts need geometry, not feature lists:** "The probe reads a continuous geometric direction in the full residual stream that the discrete feature list cannot recover." (p20)
- **The truth circuit marks falsehood:** "Its most informative features are falsehood detectors rather than truth detectors." (p7). Margin predictability "peaks at 0.66 at L12, with meaningful Ridge signal from L7 onward before decaying past L15." (p21)
- **Toxicity:** "Spearman ρ above 0.66 at every layer and peaking at 0.86 at L10." (p19)
- **Shared core:** "Top predictive features appear 6–21 times more often than a random feature across both layers" (p7).
- **Layer stability:** "The features stabilise at approximately J = 0.9 between the early intermediate layers(8-12) and remains there through the final layers." (p7)
- **Concept overlap:** for truthfulness vs reasoning, "even on the diagonal, peak values remain below 0.07, well within the low-overlap regime observed in the within-concept analysis." (p22)
- **Why a belief layer:** "A layer can therefore be linearly decodable without being the layer where the concept is actively formed." (p18)

## Controls worth copying
- **Two outcomes per intervention:** an internal ΔS *and* a behavioural flip. For us, the flip must be the correct-vs-sycophantic answer.
- **Three-way ablation with node counts:** exclusive A, exclusive B, shared. Add size-matched random ablations, which the paper lacks.
- **Guards for any cross-layer predictor:** a layer-depth baseline plus a de-meaned target.
- **Statistics:** group-aware permutation (200) and bootstrap (500).
- **Separate directions by role:** raw DIM for interventions, LDA-corrected only for accuracy.
- **Choose the layer by where the signal forms, not by peak accuracy.** Compute this on separation, and enforce the plateau rule.
- **Inputs:** balanced classes, a fixed seed, short inputs.
- **Second model family:** report sign flips honestly, as Fig 17 does.

## Pitfalls
1. **Circular orthogonality claim:** "Post-correction concept directions are near-orthogonal ... confirming that orthogonality is a property of the model’s representational geometry rather than an artefact of post-processing." (p3)
2. **Plateau rule broken:** the rule is "the belief layer is required to also fall within a 2-8% accuracy plateau of the peak" (p18). Yet Llama Truthfulness Lb = L1 at 55.2% is ~13 points below its ~68% peak (approx, Fig 7).
3. **Ablation size confound with no random-node control:** logit-exclusive subsets are 1.77-4.15× larger (p19).
4. **Ablation method unspecified:** "We then ablate graph-specific features." (p4)
5. **Possible built-in asymmetry (our inference):** logit-graph nodes above Lb cannot change S at Lb, so "ΔS≈0" is partly guaranteed. This is worst for Llama Truthfulness at L1.
6. **The behavioural outcome is an unrelated next token (our inference):** "behavioral effect as the top-1 next-token flip rate" (p18). So it does not test concept use for a concept-relevant action.
7. **Pruning stated relative to the final logit** even for probe-rooted graphs (p17).
8. **Attention is frozen:** "normalization terms, attention computations, and MLP nonlinearities, are treated as constants" (p17). So routing failures in QK, such as heads reading the user's claim, are invisible.
9. **A probe can be anti-aligned with all prompts** (Reasoning L15, p18). The signs of ΔS then invert.
10. **Text vs table:** Llama Sentiment is said to "rise toward zero" (p24), but Table 7 shows −0.47 → +2.40.
11. **Fig 15's caption is a copy of Fig 16's** and contradicts its panels (p28).
12. **The structural signature inverts between models** (p29), so it cannot be a model-agnostic monitor.
13. **Feature labels drift across sections.** L1/F3007 is described as "proper names and academic institutional contexts" (p7), "encyclopedic register" (p21), and "French proper names and French-language text" (p23). It is likely a dataset artifact, given the Spanish-English translation split.
14. **Cross-layer stability rests on one prompt per concept** (p8), and adjacent graphs share their earlier nodes by construction.
15. **The predictor has ~100 points, one per group**, so "group-aware" CV is plain CV, and neighbouring layers can leak between train and test (our inference).
16. **The enrichment baseline is a random feature, not a frequency-matched one**, and selection is partly circular (our inference).
17. **The text overstates Figure 2:** reasoning "recovering at L15" (p4) is not visible in the figure.

## Limitations and future work (authors)
- **Limitations (p9):** "CTA inherits the quality of the underlying CrossLayer Transcoder: features that the CLT conflates, omits, or represents polysemantically are invisible to attribution graphs". They also note that feature presence "discards activation magnitude".
- **Future work (p9):** "Future work should pursue broader cross-architecture replication, deeper intervention experiments targeting finegrained concept steering, and scaling the analysis to richer concept taxonomies beyond the four studied here."

## Group-geometry angle
- **Helps:**
  - It studies *sets* of features and compares them across prompts, layers and concepts. That is a discrete analogue of our subspace tracking and RSA/CKA plans.
  - Overlap is non-trivial only at matched depth (Fig 10), so we should compare pressures (H1) at matched relative depth.
  - The steepest-rise belief point can become our fork-point finder, if computed on flipped-vs-resisted separation.
- **Warns:**
  - The authors say sparse feature lists miss distributed concepts read as "a continuous geometric direction". Truthfulness is distributed: it has a weak structural signal (Fig 14), and its predictability decays after mid-depth.
  - So for correctness information, continuous group geometry (point clouds, principal angles, Procrustes) should be the main instrument, with CTA-style feature sets as a complement.
  - One-number graph summaries flipped sign between models, which warns against unvalidated scalar geometry metrics.

## Where in the architecture
- **Components:** MLP or transcoder features only. Attention is frozen, so nothing can be said about heads.
- **Gemma-2-2B, 26 layers:**
  - Truthfulness forms at L14 (about mid-depth). Accuracy peaks around L11-13 and margin predictability at L12. Key features sit at L9 (falsehood markers), plus a lexical L1 feature.
  - Toxicity rests on L0 lexical features, with the belief layer at L22.
  - Sentiment (L21) and reasoning (L15) form late.
- **Across models:** the concept-vs-generation separation shows up in both models. Truthfulness location does **not** replicate (Llama Lb = L1, near-chance probe), and the graph signatures invert.
- **Not tested:** seeds, checkpoints, or anything above 2B.

## Actionable fix it implies
- **Monitor:** overlap between the correct-answer probe circuit and the answer-logit circuit on flipped items. Low overlap means the decodable signal is not reaching the decision. The authors' "probe-free" topology monitor is model-specific, so do not trust it without per-model validation.
- **Intervention:** for us, target the *shared* nodes where truth information enters generation; in truthfulness these control both S and the token. In Stage B, check after DPO whether the sycophantic-answer logit graph is disconnected (masked) or gone.
- **Caveat:** the paper demonstrates none of this for a concept-relevant action.

## Reuse verdict
**Adapt.** Copy the ΔS-plus-flip, three-subset ablation design and the CV, permutation and bootstrap controls. Change four things:
1. use the answer flip as the behaviour;
2. add size-matched random ablations;
3. truncate the logit graph to layers at or below the probe layer;
4. pair it with activation patching for attention routing.

Avoid the structural probe-quality predictor and the belief-point definition as written. A practical blocker remains: it needs CLTs or transcoders at 9-27B, which the paper does not supply.
