# 2605.14038 · Model-Adaptive Tool Necessity Reveals the Knowing-Doing Gap in LLM Tool Use

- Authors: Cheng, Fan, JafariRaviz, Rezaei, Feizi (Maryland). 2026 (v3 read).
- Date read: 2026-11-10
- status: full (main text §1-6, App. A-F). Figures not viewed (text only), so flow sizes in Fig 6 are not checked.
- Why it matters here: the paper Dr. Lin pointed to as the kind of knowing-vs-doing work she likes (meeting 2026-11-09).

## Question
When a model needs a tool to answer reliably, does it register that internally, and does it then actually call the tool?

## Method
- Model-adaptive necessity: a tool is "necessary" for model f on query x unless f answers x correctly in all N=10 no-tool runs at T=0.7.
- Models: Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-4B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct; Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct for probes only (App. D).
- Data: 4,000 generated arithmetic problems (calculator tool); TruthfulQA, 817 items (search tool). Tool-call action collected with greedy decoding.
- "Knowing" = a linear probe w_c trained on the necessity label from hidden states (all layers, last 20 query tokens), scored by MCC on a 30% held-out split. "Doing" = the tool call, plus a second linear probe w_a for it.
- Per-item tracing Factual -> Cognition (probe at last token, last layer) -> Action.

## Key claims
- [P1] Necessity-action mismatch is 26.5-54.0% on arithmetic and 30.8-41.8% on TruthfulQA (Table 1, "End-to-end mismatch is substantial") verified
- [P2] The failure direction is not uniform: Qwen3-8B over-calls on arithmetic (UN-C 38.2%) but under-calls on TruthfulQA (§4.2, "no single, uniform bias can fully explain these mismatch errors") verified
- [P3] Necessity is linearly decodable but task-dependent: broad MCC >= 0.4 on arithmetic, much smaller region on TruthfulQA (§5.1, "Linear separability of necessity is strongly task-dependent") verified
- [P4] On arithmetic the necessity signal may track surface form (§5.1, "problem difficulty is reflected in the input's surface structure") verified
- [P5] The action is highly decodable, and steering along w_a corrects 66.2-100% of misaligned cases (§5.2; App. C, Table 5) verified
- [P6] cos(w_c, w_a) is near zero at the last token in late layers; at 70B the final-layer value is -0.018 (arithmetic) and 0.026 (TruthfulQA) (§5.3; App. D) verified
- [P7] Most errors are probe-right, action-wrong (§5.4, "Stage two carries the majority of error"), and they sit at probe confidence near 0 or 1, not near 0.5 (§5.4, Fig 7) verified (text only)
- [P8] The only fix is at inference time: probe-guided decoding (force or mask the tool-call token) cuts the misaligned rate by 34.67-77.35% relative on held-out data (§5.4, Table 2) verified
- [P9] The cognition probe is not causally tested (App. C, "the probing result on the cognition probe remains correlational") verified
- [P10] Asking the model yes/no whether a tool is needed predicts necessity poorly (MCC <= 0.23) and changes its tool behaviour on up to 49.27% of items (App. E, Table 6) verified
- [P11] Limits they state: only arithmetic and TruthfulQA with simple tools; multi-turn agentic settings left open (App. F, "relatively restricted") verified

## Gaps (my reading)
- "Knowing" is a probe trained on the ground-truth label: decodable is not the same as used. No surface-feature or logit-margin baseline for the probe.
- The near-zero cosine compares two separately trained logistic-regression weight vectors. No random-pair baseline is reported in the text, and probe weights are filters, not patterns (2611.07071 App. C.1), so the "orthogonal" evidence is weak.
- No training-time fix, and no check of whether any fix changes the internal knowing -> action link.
- No account of where the gap comes from (which post-training stage or data).
- No test of whether the necessity direction transfers across the two domains (not seen in the text).
