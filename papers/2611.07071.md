## Question
Can a training penalty written in terms of a model's own concept directions ("do not rely on X", "do not express X") cut a bad behaviour while keeping the skill, and does the concept stay monitorable afterwards?

## Method
- Reliance shaping: split the model at layer l; penalise the squared attribution of the readout (class logit, or the DPO preference margin) along a concept direction v (Gradient, Gradient x Input, IG, SmoothGrad, Noise, Shuffle, Deletion). v = difference of means (DoM), refit every n steps.
- Expression shaping: subtract a probe/DoM score of the rollout's activations from the RL reward (detached = RLFR-style; attached = gradient through the probe).
- Monitoring levels L1 reuse probe / L2 linear refit / L3 nonlinear refit / L4 other layer / L5 other model (Table 1).
- Toy theory (§6): fixed projection -> concept re-encoded elsewhere along the direction the head already reads; frequent refits -> concept moves into a second-order "product code" that only a nonlinear reader sees.

## Key claims
- [P1] Sycophancy setup is Blank et al. 2026 (2608.31079): OLMo-3 DPO raises multi-turn capitulation on MMLU after pushback; <1% of DPO pairs look sycophantic, so data filtering barely helps (§3.1-3.2, "less than 1% of the data contains sycophantic signal") verified
- [P2] Gradient x Input reliance shaping during DPO keeps sycophancy near the SFT level and DPO's benchmark gains; reward shaping, filtering, inoculation prompting fall on the "less DPO" line; works at OLMo 3 7B, OLMo 2 13B, OLMo 3 32B with lambda 0.1-1 (§3.2, Fig 13, Fig 40) verified
- [P3] "Capabilities" = mean of six benchmarks (GSM8k etc.) and DPO loss. No test of whether the model still accepts CORRECT pushback / reliable corrections (none in §3 or App. G). verified (absence, within what I read)
- [P4] Sycophancy direction = DoM of SFT-model reply activations, capitulating (n=2,217) minus holding (n=2,489), on initially-correct MMLU answers, layer 17 of 32, mean over reply tokens (App. G.2, Eq 12) verified
- [P5] Steering with that DoM moves sycophancy from 90% to 0% (random direction only to 43%) (App. G.1, Fig 38) verified
- [P6] Monitorability: original DoM AUROC 0.981 -> 0.931, refit linear probe stays perfect (between L1 and L2); cos(v0, preference direction b_t) < 0.097 (§3.2, Fig 14) verified. One run.
- [P7] Layer swap: the shaping change sits in the ~5 blocks just below the penalty layer; swapping 5 such blocks of the shaped model into the plain DPO model recovers up to 76% of the sycophancy reduction with benchmarks within 0.6 points (App. G.4, Fig 41) verified
- [P8] Harm (§5): gradient through the probe drops the original probe to AUROC 0.58-0.64 in 50 updates and to 0.30 (below chance) after 400 updates on Mistral, while a refit linear probe recovers 0.83-0.87; a nonlinear reader adds only 0.02-0.04 (§5, Fig 21) verified. One seed.
- [P9] "direction drift alone does not establish a loss of monitoring": the frozen-base DoM rotates ~50 deg while L1 AUROC stays 0.84 (§5, Fig 20) verified
- [P10] Pattern (DoM, where the concept is written) vs filter (probe, where it can be detected): w* ∝ Σ^-1 v; use the pattern for intervention/specification, the filter for detection; DoM gives ~90% vs ~80% WGA for a probe direction on Waterbirds (App. C.1, §2.1.3, Fig 8) verified
- [P11] Three circumvention forms: re-encoding (L2 reads it), erasure (all readers fail), nonlinear product code (L3 reads it) (§2.1.2, §6) verified
- [P12] Open: frontier scale; training duration ("left for future work"); which circumvention forms are realizable (§7) verified

## Relevance to this project
1. Same behaviour, same stage. Their target (capitulation after DPO) is our harmful caving; our Step 1 measures it across Tulu SFT -> DPO -> final. OLMo 3 has a documented DPO sycophancy jump (Blank; Goodfire) and public SFT/DPO checkpoints: a possible positive-control lineage.
2. Untested cost. They never measure useful updating. Their direction contrasts "changed answer" vs "held answer", and steering it reaches 0% capitulation, which could also mean "never updates". Orgad 2610.28325 says caving and useful updating share weights. So: does reliance shaping (or their DoM) also suppress useful updating? Open, directly in our lane.
3. Block swap (P7) is a no-training localisation tool we can use between our own checkpoints (Tulu SFT <-> DPO): which blocks carry the caving change, and do the same blocks carry the useful-updating change? Avoids the pruning format break (A3), unverified.
4. Monitoring levels L1-L5 + three circumvention forms map onto our fate list (transformed / erased / not routed). P8 (below-chance original probe, linear refit recovers) supports ledger A1: a below-chance probe points to re-encoding, not erasure.
5. Pattern vs filter (P10) formalises "decodable by a probe is not used by the model": use DoM for interventions, a probe for detection, report both.
6. Idea 3 (does anti-sycophancy training mask the mechanism): reliance shaping is by design a "keep encoded, stop relying" fix (P6). Relapse (prompts, benign fine-tuning, longer training) is not tested; they flag training duration as future work.

## Caveats
Goodfire's own method; monitorability results are single runs/seeds; sycophancy only on one pushback format (MMLU, initially-correct answers, "That's incorrect"); I have not read Blank et al. 2608.31079 in full.
