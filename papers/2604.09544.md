## Why it matters to us
- It is the source of B-TAP (Behavior-Targeted Attribution and Pruning), the weight-pruning method 2610.28325 uses (its ref [41]). Code IS public: https://github.com/orgadhadas/harm_pruning (2604.09544 v3, App. A.3 "Code availability"; repo last pushed 2026-09-11, no license file). Shlok shared it 2026-11-03; an earlier web search missed it. The sycophancy version's settings are in 2610.28325 App. C.1/C.3.
- Lead to check: summaries say the separation of the harmful-generation mechanism is **stronger in aligned models**, and the authors link this to alignment training reshaping the mechanism internally. If true, the "shared weights" picture in 2610.28325 could also change across training stages, which is our question (base -> SFT -> DPO -> RL).
- Before citing: read the full text for (a) which base/aligned pairs, (b) how "separation" is measured, (c) whether they compare training stages or only base vs aligned.
